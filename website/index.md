### About me

<article>
Hej! I'm a software engineer based in Sweden.

My weapons of choice are [Haskell](https://www.haskell.org/) and [Nix](https://nixos.org/), but I also have experience with a variety of other languages and tools. My professional interests include automated testing, programming languages design and implementation, digital electronics, low-level programming, web development, among many others.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Chalmers University of Technology](https://chalmers.se), where I conducted [my research](/pubs) under the guidance of [Alejandro Russo](http://www.cse.chalmers.se/~russo). Before that, I received a Licentiate Degree in Computer Science from the [National University of Rosarion](https://unr.edu.ar).

I'm currently working as a Haskell engineer at [Tweag](https://tweag.io) (part of [Modus Create](https://moduscreate.com)) on highly-specialized projects. Previously, I focused on developing reproducible build environments at [Zenseact](https://zenseact.com) and worked at [DPella](https://dpella.io), where I helped develop the pilot version of their custom [differentially-private](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_privacy) programming language.

I also write about [random stuff](/blog) sometimes. Not as much as I'd like, though.

</article>
